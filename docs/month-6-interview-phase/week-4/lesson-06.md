# Deep build — negotiation prep + the course retrospective

**Week 4 · Deep build · ~3 hrs** · Month 6 — Interview Phase

## Objective
Close the course: build the negotiation pack (level-anchored: target level, the evidence for it, comp research from the goal's sources with dates, walk-away logic) and write the full course retrospective — six months, one system, from frontend to agentic-AI engineer.

## Why this exists
Negotiation is anchored to *level*, not just comp (the research's explicit advice): the pack assembles your levelling evidence (scope, systems, measured outcomes) beside dated market data — and the retrospective closes the course the way Month 1 taught you to close a day: what was built, what broke, what you can now do unaided.

Readiness gate passed; applications live.

## Prerequisite check
Re-run the previous lesson's independent verification (Month 6 Week 4, Lesson 5 (Consolidation — final readiness gate)) before starting. If it no longer passes, repair first and compress today's objective — never stack new work on a broken base.

## Core concept
Negotiation pack rules: use the goal research's dated figures (Levels.fyi medians as indexed Oct 2026 — flag them as dated and re-verify live bands per posting before any real negotiation; the pack records *sources + dates*, not just numbers); realistic-switcher anchoring (senior-track-but-new-domain levelling — big-tech roughly L4/L5-equivalent TC bands from the research, startup base + equity valued at cash + harsh probability discount, never headline valuation); the level case is argued from scope artefacts (platform, deployment, security) — the same evidence as the loops.

## Build steps
Expected output is stated *before* you run each step. If you see something else, stop, classify the error (slip / execution-model / API misconception / design misconception), and check the failure notes.

### Step 1 — Assemble market data (dated)
Compile the pack's comp section from the goal research + any live posting bands for your active applications; every figure carries source + date.

**Expected output:** Comp section committed; no undated number survives.

### Step 2 — Write the level case
One page: the level you're targeting per company type + the scope evidence (systems owned, measured outcomes, deployment) that supports it.

**Expected output:** Level case committed; every claim traces to the evidence packs.

### Step 3 — Walk-away + trade logic
Write your decision rules in advance: what trades against what (level vs comp vs team fit vs remote), and the conditions that end a negotiation.

**Expected output:** Rules committed — written now, while no offer is on the table, by design.

### Step 4 — The course retrospective
Six-month retro in the Month 1 format, at full altitude: spend vs the six budget columns, the system's final state, the judgement review (three reversals + the defence, updated from Month 5), and the post-course backlog (open gaps, systematic market gaps, the next build).

**Expected output:** Retrospective committed + tagged as the course's closing artefact; PROGRESS.md gets its final Month 6 entries.

## Independent verification (checkpoint)
